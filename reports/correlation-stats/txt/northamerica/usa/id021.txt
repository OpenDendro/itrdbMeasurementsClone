   the segment is weak wherever it is placed, and the shift is not evidence of a dating error.
   Treat it as a low correlation, like an A.
 Averages in the summary and the totals are weighted by the number of years in each series, as
   COFECHA's are.
 Unfiltered block and mean sensitivity: the measurements as read, and sens1().
 dplR filtered block: the series that was correlated, after filtering. COFECHA's filtered
   series is defined differently, so these three columns are not COFECHA's numbers.
 AR: order of the AR model used to prewhiten.

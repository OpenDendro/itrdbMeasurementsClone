Bison, Northwest Territories, Canada - cana295  
Additional Information 
Andy Bunn, Andrea Lloyd, and Logan Berner  


Purpose of Collection: 
The purpose of this collection was to characterize 
stand-level tree growth patterns for comparison with 
satellite-derived vegetation indices.

Cores and cross-sections are stored at: 
Western Washington University, Bellingham, Washington, USA. 

Reference: 
Berner, L.B. 2010. 
Evaluation of high-latitude forest growth using
satellite-derived vegetation indices. 
M.S. Thesis, Western Washington University.  
Available from WWU Masters Thesis Collection: 
http://content.wwu.edu/u?/theses,343.

Notes: 
Patches of spruce and small Larix laricina interspersed 
with bog, grass tussocks, dwarf betula and salix.
